Questions, answered

A few things you might be wondering.

Want to know more? Reach out to the WalkReach team through the onboarding flow.

What is WalkReach?

WalkReach is a people-discovery and connection concept designed to help people discover others across different distances, connect through messaging, use Travel Mode, and explore businesses.

Can I discover people outside my immediate area?

WalkReach offers different radius options. Some wider-radius options require an eligible pass or subscription. The feed on this page is simulated.

Is WalkReach free?

The supplied product rules include free discovery options alongside paid passes, subscriptions, and business listing packages.

Does this website create a real account?

No. This is a frontend demonstration. You can use the WhatsApp onboarding flow to express interest in joining, but no account is created on this page.

Are my password or verification codes sent through WhatsApp?

No. The onboarding flow never asks for or sends passwords, verification codes, or authentication secrets. WhatsApp opens with a message you can review and send yourself.

Does the demo messaging send to real people?

No. Messaging and all profiles shown here are simulated. A production version needs a secure backend and real messaging infrastructure.
