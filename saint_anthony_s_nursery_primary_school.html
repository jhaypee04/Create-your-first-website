Welcome to Saint Anthony's

Building Strong Foundations for Academic & Moral Excellence

Located in Kabusa Village, Along Sheretti Road, Kabusa, Abuja, Nigeria, Saint Anthony's Nursery/Primary & Day Secondary School has earned a reputation for nurturing well-rounded pupils equipped for future academic challenges.

Our holistic educational approach seamlessly merges rigorous academics with extracurricular enrichment, sound moral instruction, and modern computer literacy. We provide a safe haven where young minds explore, discover, and build confidence daily.
